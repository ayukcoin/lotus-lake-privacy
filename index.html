Privacy Policy for Lotus Lake

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Lotus Lake (“the game”) is published by AyukLab (“we”).

The short version

The game does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information, from anyone of any age. It works without an internet connection and does not ask for any device permissions.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress on your own device only: which levels you have unlocked, the stars you earned on each level, the coins and Auras you have collected, and whether sound is switched on. This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in your device settings.

If your device has Android’s own backup turned on, Android may include this progress in your personal device backup. That backup is run by Google under your Google account, not by us.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address or any account.
  • It does not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or files.
  • It does not contain advertising.
  • It does not contain analytics, tracking or any third-party data-collection tools.
  • It does not connect to the internet.
  • It does not offer purchases. Coins in the game are earned only by playing and cannot be bought.
  • It has no chat and no way for players to contact each other.

Children

Lotus Lake is suitable for children, and we intend it to be enjoyed by players of all ages, including children under 13.

Because the game collects no personal information at all, it does not collect personal information from children. There is nothing for a parent to review or delete on our side: everything the game remembers stays on the device and can be removed by uninstalling the game or clearing its data.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes any of the above, for example by adding online features, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the “Last updated” date will change.
